No such sheet

That address does not name anything in this cabinet. Nothing is broken on your end, and nothing here is lost — the address simply does not correspond to a sheet.

Three ways this happens Link to this section

An address was typed with a character out of place, and the sheet it nearly names is a click away below. Or a link somewhere else on the web points at something we moved or never had — if you followed one, the fault is ours and we would like to know. Or you reached for a sheet that has not been mounted yet, which is the most likely case on a site of eleven pages that is two days old.

Every address here is permanent once published. The accession register records every sheet as it was mounted and every label we corrected, so if something used to be at this address, that is the book it is written in. Nothing has been withdrawn.

Where to look instead Link to this section

There is no search box here, and that is deliberate. With eleven pages, the plate is the index: the front page lays out every sheet as a numbered card, which is faster to read than any query would be. When there is enough here to need searching, that will be worth building and this paragraph will be wrong.

Otherwise: the colophon explains how this site is made — the herbarium sheet, the palette, the type, and the borrowed plates. The register is the ledger. Every sheet is carded below.

If a link sent you here, say so in the repository and it becomes a register entry. A broken inbound link is a fact about this site, not about the reader who followed it.
