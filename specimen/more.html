Font specimen, second sheet

The families named in conversation that the first sheet could not carry — the Bye Bye Binary faces whose names are the inclusive endings themselves, and Velvetyne, whose own site timed out on every request so these come from their GitHub. Each block overrides only --qe-display and --qe-body, which is exactly what a picker would do — so what you see here is what you would get. Toggle the ground; body text is 1.19rem at 1.65, the same as a sheet.

→ First sheet: Redaction Inclusive, Unormative Fraktur, Atkinson

This is a scratch page. It is noindex, it is not in sitemap.xml, and it should be deleted once the choice is made — a specimen kept after the decision is a second, stale account of what the site’s type is. Regenerate with node tools/make-specimen.mjs. Licences were fetched with the files; two families state their terms only in a README and are marked as such, which needs reading before either ships.

Glyphs the grammar refuses

BBB Baskervvol

Bye Bye Binary · CUTE (Collective Use, Transformation, Emancipation) · licence · 124 KB · could do either

A Baskerville carrying inclusive ligatures — the one I most wanted on the first sheet and could not find, because the repo hides its OTFs behind two hundred UFO glyph folders. Baskerville is about as canonical as a text face gets; recutting it to say what the grammar refuses is the argument made inside the canon rather than against it.

Adelphe

Bye Bye Binary · stated in the README, no separate licence file upstream · licence · 61 KB · proposed for display

“Adelphe” is the French neutral for sibling — the word coined because frère and sœur leave nowhere to stand. The cut here is Floréal, after the revolutionary calendar’s month of flowering, which lands oddly well on a herbarium sheet.
