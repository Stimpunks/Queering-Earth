Font specimen, second sheet

The families named in conversation that the first sheet could not carry — the Bye Bye Binary faces whose names are the inclusive endings themselves, and Velvetyne, whose own site timed out on every request so these come from their GitHub. Each block overrides only --qe-display and --qe-body, which is exactly what a picker would do — so what you see here is what you would get. Toggle the ground; body text is 1.19rem at 1.65, the same as a sheet.

→ First sheet: Redaction Inclusive, Unormative Fraktur, Atkinson

Nine of these are now in the picker under Reading, and they render here from the very files the site serves — not a second download of the same family, so what you judge is exactly what a reader gets. That also corrected three faces shown in the wrong weight on the first pass: Adelphe as Floréal Bold, Coxinel·le as Black and Sporting Grotesque as Bold, because those filenames sorted first in their repositories. Two full text families presented as heavy display faces. The rest are marked not kept and stay for comparison.

This is a scratch page. It is noindex, it is not in sitemap.xml, and it should be deleted once the choice is made — a specimen kept after the decision is a second, stale account of what the site’s type is. Regenerate with node tools/make-specimen.mjs. Licences were fetched with the files, and every candidate links its original publication source — which CUTE requires and the OFL merely encourages.

The Bye Bye Binary families are under CUTE, not the OFL, and it is not a permissive licence with a different name. It permits use, modification and commercial use; it also states outright that it is not technically compatible with any FLOSS license to date. Three of its conditions bind anything shipped from here: credit and link the source; share complete files rather than a lone .otf; and do not delete the post-binary characters or the OpenType features that activate them — which forbids the latin-only subsetting the two production faces get. It also asks for a donation on a sliding scale as a condition rather than a suggestion. None of that rules the fonts out; all of it has to be decided rather than assumed.

BBB Baskervvol

Bye Bye Binary · source · CUTE (Collective Use, Transformation, Emancipation) · licence · 124 KB · sets both faces

A Baskerville carrying inclusive ligatures — the one I most wanted on the first sheet and could not find, because the repo hides its OTFs behind two hundred UFO glyph folders. Baskerville is about as canonical as a text face gets; recutting it to say what the grammar refuses is the argument made inside the canon rather than against it.

Adelphe

Bye Bye Binary · source · stated in the README, no separate licence file upstream · licence · 60 KB · sets both faces

“Adelphe” is the French neutral for sibling — the word coined because frère and sœur leave nowhere to stand. The cut here is Floréal, after the revolutionary calendar’s month of flowering, which lands oddly well on a herbarium sheet.
