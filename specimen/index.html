Font specimen

Candidates for a font picker, set in this site’s own palette at its own sizes, on its own paper. Each block overrides only --qe-display and --qe-body, which is exactly what a picker would do — so what you see here is what you would get. Toggle the ground; body text is 1.19rem at 1.65, the same as a sheet.

→ Second sheet: Baskervvol, Velvetyne, and the inclusive suffixes

This is a scratch page. It is noindex, it is not in sitemap.xml, and it should be deleted once the choice is made — a specimen kept after the decision is a second, stale account of what the site’s type is. Regenerate with node tools/make-specimen.mjs. Licences were fetched with the files, and every candidate links its original publication source — which CUTE requires and the OFL merely encourages.

The Bye Bye Binary families are under CUTE, not the OFL, and it is not a permissive licence with a different name. It permits use, modification and commercial use; it also states outright that it is not technically compatible with any FLOSS license to date. Three of its conditions bind anything shipped from here: credit and link the source; share complete files rather than a lone .otf; and do not delete the post-binary characters or the OpenType features that activate them — which forbids the latin-only subsetting the two production faces get. It also asks for a donation on a sliding scale as a condition rather than a suggestion. None of that rules the fonts out; all of it has to be decided rather than assumed.
