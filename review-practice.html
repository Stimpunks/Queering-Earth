    Queering Earth

    Leaving notes on a draft

    This is a practice sheet on the drafts branch. It is not published, it is not in the register, and nothing you do to it can reach anybody — which is the point. Try the tool here first, on a page that does not matter.

    On this sheet

      What you are looking at Link to this section

      Everything at this address is a draft. It is the real site — the same typefaces, the same paper, the same cabinet when you shut the drawer — served from a branch that has not been published. A sheet sits here while it is being argued about, and goes to queering.earth only when it is finished.

      The banner at the top of the page is the only thing here that will not exist on the published sheet. It disappears when the draft is mounted, along with every note anybody left on it.

      Leaving a note Link to this section

      Press Annotate in the banner. Every paragraph, heading, list item and quotation grows a small Note button on its right. Press one, write whatever you would have said out loud, and press Save note. The note appears under the paragraph it belongs to, and you can edit or delete it afterwards.

      Press Stop annotating when you want to read without the buttons in the way. The notes you have already left stay where they are.

      A quotation takes one note rather than one per line, because a quotation is one object:

      Considering how common illness is, how tremendous the spiritual change that it brings, how astonishing, when the lights of health go down, the undiscovered countries that are then disclosed … it becomes strange indeed that illness has not taken its place with love and battle and jealousy among the prime themes of literature.

      Virginia Woolf, On Being Ill, 1926

      Try it on that one. The same is true of the specimen table at the head of a sheet: it is one object and takes one note.

      Sending them back Link to this section

      Press Copy my notes. That puts all of them on your clipboard as plain text, each one carrying the section it was in, a link straight to that section, and the sentence you were looking at. Paste it into a message and send it — email, Signal, wherever the two of you already talk.

      Nothing is sent anywhere on its own. Until you paste them, your notes exist only in this browser on this device.

      Three things worth knowing Link to this section

      Your notes survive closing the tab, so you can review half a sheet on Tuesday and the rest on Thursday. They do not travel between devices, and a second reviewer on the same draft has their own set that you will never see.

      If the paragraph under one of your notes is rewritten while you are away, the note stays put and says the text here has changed since this note was written. It is never quietly dropped, and it still copies out.

      If a Note button is missing somewhere it ought to be, or lands somewhere it ought not, say so — that is a fault in the tool and not in the way you are using it.
